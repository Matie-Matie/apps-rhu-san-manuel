# APPS

## Register

product

## Users

**Doctors** — Create and manage prescriptions, view patient history, generate reports. They are in a clinical workflow: diagnose, prescribe, review. Need speed and clarity, not decoration.

**Pharmacists** — Validate prescriptions, manage medicine inventory, dispense medications, monitor stock levels. They are in a logistics + verification workflow: check stock, validate, dispense, reorder.

Both use the system in a busy RHU (Rural Health Unit) clinic in San Manuel, Isabela. Screens are viewed under clinic lighting on desktops/laptops. Users need to move fast between tasks.

## Product Purpose

Replace paper-based prescription and inventory tracking with a clean digital system. Success means: doctors write prescriptions faster, pharmacists never accidentally dispense out-of-stock items, and the clinic has accurate records for reporting.

## Brand Personality

Professional, clean, capable. Not cold or sterile. Not friendly or playful. The tone is that of a well-run clinic — organized, attentive, no-nonsense.

Three words: **Capable. Clean. Precise.**

## Anti-references

- Do NOT look like a generic hospital system (heavy, cluttered, gray).
- Do NOT look like a consumer health app (too friendly, illustrated, rounded-everything).
- Do NOT use bright neon colors or playful design elements.
- No emojis anywhere.
- No "glassmorphism" or decorative blur effects.
- No left-stripe borders on cards (the h-2 colored bar pattern).

## Design Principles

1. **The tool disappears into the task.** Every screen prioritizes the work, not the chrome. Navigation is predictable, actions are where users expect them.
2. **Role-aware, not role-locked.** Doctors and pharmacists see what they need, never what they don't. The interface adapts without calling attention to itself.
3. **Clinical confidence.** Clean typography, deliberate spacing, restrained color. The system earns trust through consistency, not decoration.
4. **Offline-first, always.** The PWA guarantee means this works in a clinic with unreliable internet. The UI never assumes connectivity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- System fonts for native platform familiarity.
- Sufficient color contrast for clinic lighting conditions.
- Keyboard-navigable forms and tables.
- Status indicators use both color AND text/icon (not color-only).
